# Menu Bar Icon

blind's icon in the macOS menu bar lists your Open and Closed Sessions, so any of them is one click away. Choosing a Closed Session reopens it; see [Sessions](sessions.md#closed-sessions).

Its menu also offers New Session, Open Settings, Quit Blind and [Quit and End Shells](shells-outlive-the-app.md#quit-and-end-shells).

## Settings

- [`menuBarIcon`](../configuration/settings.md#menubaricon): whether the icon follows the menu bar's colour or keeps its own, which the Change Menu Bar Icon action also sets.
